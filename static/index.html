    How it works
    
      
    Every task starts as a permission slip that lists the files, memory, CPU, time and websites it may use, and nothing runs until you approve it. The approved work then runs in a locked, throwaway container with a read-only system, no secrets and hard resource limits. It can reach only the websites listed on the slip, through a proxy that refuses everything else. When it finishes, you get a report of what it used and what was blocked, and the container is removed.
